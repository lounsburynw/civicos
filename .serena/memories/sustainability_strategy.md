# Civic Sustainability Strategy

**Captured:** Session 481 (Jan 2026)

## Core Principle

Sustainable funding is a feature of the mission, not a betrayal of it. Unfunded projects die or get acquired by misaligned entities (e.g., Open States → PE-backed SAI360, Dec 2025).

## Cost Reality

Single municipality: ~$150-350/mo (Supabase, AssemblyAI, Modal, AI providers)
Ten cities with scale: ~$500-1000/mo
Not "$7/mo" but bootstrappable.

## Recommended Model: Freemium

**Free tier (civic right):**
- What's happening, who represents me, basic search, public records
- Core API: `whats_next()`, `what_happened()`, `what_applies()`

**Paid tier (civic power):**
- `prepare()` - testimony drafting
- `whos_with_me()` - coalition finding
- Property/topic alerts
- Priority transcription
- Professional API access

## Strategic Principles

1. Free tier is non-negotiable
2. Paid features must deliver real value (not artificial scarcity)
3. Avoid VC—exit pressure corrupts mission
4. Price for sustainability, not extraction
5. Municipal contracts as anchor revenue

## Key Insight

People pay when they have skin in the game: property values, development fights, business permits. The `prepare()` method is the most obvious monetization candidate.

## Open Source Strategy

- License: AGPL-3.0 (copyleft, prevents proprietary forks)
- Model: WordPress-style (code free, operated service is product)
- Contributor deal: Municipal parsers → free premium access for that city
- Corporate structure: Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) to protect mission

The scaling strategy (community parsers) still works because:
- Parsers are infrastructure, not product
- Product is fresh, reliable, *operated* data
- Contributors get network effects + premium access
- PBC signals contributors won't be PE-exited
