# Phase 2: Meeting Agenda & Participation Analysis

**Completed**: 2024-11-24
**Goal**: Understand what drives participation variance across 9 San Rafael meetings

---

## Executive Summary

**Key Finding**: **Homelessness/camping policy dominated civic participation** - the two highest-engagement meetings (April 15: 714 utterances, Aug 19: 679 utterances) both featured camping ordinances and homelessness programs.

**Oct 7 Mystery Solved**: The meeting with lowest participation (83 utterances, 5 speakers) was a **special meeting for Planning Commission interviews** - not a regular policy meeting. This explains the dramatic drop from typical 8-10 speakers.

**Pattern**: High-stakes, controversial topics (displacement, homelessness, housing development) drive 3-4x more participation than routine business (budget reports, fee schedules, MOUs).

## Key Insights

### 1. Homelessness Policy Dominates Participation
- April 15 + Aug 19 = 39% of all testimony despite being 2 of 9 meetings
- Camping ordinances drew 3-4x more per-speaker engagement than baseline
- This is the most controversial topic in San Rafael (March-Oct 2024)

### 2. High-Stakes Decisions ≠ Always High Participation
- Budget meetings (June 3, June 17) had moderate engagement despite being consequential
- Grand Jury responses (July 15) are routine obligations, not decision points
- Final budget votes may have lower testimony if controversy resolved in preliminary discussions

### 3. Special Meetings Are Not Comparable
- Oct 7 special meeting skews participation metrics
- Should be excluded from "typical" participation analysis
- Explains the apparent drop from 8-10 speakers to 5 speakers

### 4. Speaker Count Stability, Engagement Variance
- Speaker count fairly stable (8-10 for regular meetings)
- **Engagement per speaker** varies 4x (21 to 85 utterances/speaker)
- **Implication**: Same people show up, but controversial topics make them speak more/longer
