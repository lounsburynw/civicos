Quick Reference

1. Monochrome: Safest. No brand confusion possible. Pure infrastructure.

2. Orange Only: Keeps urgency, loses Citibank by dropping blue.

3. Cyan: Calm, technical, "open source" feel.

4. Green: Civic, sustainable, earthy.

5. Violet: Distinctive, unexpected, less corporate.

6. Red: Political urgency. Maybe too aggressive?

7. Pure Solarized Base: Maximum developer credibility. Understated.

8. Outside Solarized: Fresh options if you want to break from the palette.
