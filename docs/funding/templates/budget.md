## Notes for Grant Applications

### What's NOT Included
- Founder salary replacement (for small/medium grants)
- Office space (working remotely)
- Legal/accounting (using existing resources)
- Hardware (using personal equipment)

### Cost Efficiency Narrative
> "Our current operational cost is <$7/month for 26 cities. This grant would accelerate
> development and pilot execution, not sustain ongoing operations. The infrastructure
> is designed to be sustainable at minimal cost."

### Sustainability Narrative
> "After grant period, the platform continues to operate at <$100/month. We're seeking
> funding to validate the model, not to create dependency on ongoing grants."

### Open Source Commitment
> "All code developed with grant funding will remain open source (MIT license),
> enabling other regions to replicate the model."
