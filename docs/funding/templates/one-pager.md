# Civic: Coordination Infrastructure for Local Democracy

## The Problem

High-stakes municipal decisions are made without resident input—not because people don't care, but because they lack coordination infrastructure.

- **780+ agenda items/month** across Bay Area cities, buried in PDFs and government portals
- **5-hour council meetings** with no way to find "what was said about my neighborhood"
- **Affected residents don't know each other** and can't coordinate testimony
- **Result:** Policy shaped by organized interests, not community voice

ChatGPT can research any topic. It cannot organize your neighbors.

## The Solution

**Civic** is AI-enabled infrastructure that detects high-stakes decisions, identifies affected residents, and orchestrates coordinated participation.

### What We've Built

| Capability | Status | Impact |
|------------|--------|--------|
| Multi-platform event extraction | Live | 26 cities, 780+ items/month |
| Meeting transcript search | Working | 5-hour meeting → 30-second lookup |
| Legislative context matching | Working | Connect local decisions to state/federal policy |
| Resident coordination workflow | Pilot ready | Route affected residents to relevant decisions |

**Operational cost:** <$7/month for 26 cities

### What We're Validating

**Core hypothesis:** If we identify affected residents and help them coordinate before a decision, they will feel more empowered to influence outcomes.

**Pilot:** San Rafael, January 2026
- Identify one high-stakes decision (budget >$500K or policy change)
- Find 5-10 affected residents via complaint data + geography
- Facilitate pre-meeting coordination session
- Measure: >50% report feeling "more capable of influencing the outcome"

## Traction

- **26 cities** with live event extraction (Legistar, CivicClerk, Granicus, CivicPlus)
- **1,340 resident complaints** linked to policy decisions (San Rafael SeeClickFix)
- **285 searchable transcript chunks** from Nov 2025 council meeting (5+ hours → instant search)
- **$7/month** operational cost proves sustainability at scale
