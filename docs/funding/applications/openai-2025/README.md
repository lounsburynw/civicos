## Draft Application

### Project Summary

Civic uses AI to make local democracy accessible. We process hundreds of municipal agenda items monthly, extract actionable information from city council meetings, and match affected residents with decisions that impact their lives.

**The AI components:**
- **Document understanding:** Extract structured data from PDFs, HTML, and video transcripts
- **Semantic search:** Enable "what was said about [topic]" queries over hours of meeting footage
- **Legislative matching:** Connect local decisions to relevant state bills and federal programs
- **Coordination routing:** Identify which residents are affected by which decisions

**Why AI is essential:** A single Bay Area region generates 780+ agenda items per month across 26 cities. No human team can review this volume. AI makes civic intelligence at scale economically viable (<$7/month for 26 cities).

### How This Advances OpenAI's Mission

OpenAI's mission is to ensure AGI benefits all of humanity. Local democracy is where most policy decisions actually happen—zoning, budgets, public safety, schools. By making these decisions accessible and enabling coordination, we demonstrate AI as a tool for democratic empowerment, not just productivity.

**The narrative:** "AI helped residents organize around a shelter decision, resulting in better policy outcomes for both the housed and unhoused community."
