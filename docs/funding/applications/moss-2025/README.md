## Draft Application

### Project Description (max 500 words)

Civic is open-source infrastructure for civic coordination at the local level. We extract municipal decision data from 5+ platforms (Legistar, CivicClerk, Granicus, CivicPlus, SeeClickFix) and make it searchable, enriched with legislative context, and connected to affected residents.

**The problem:** Local democracy suffers from an information and coordination gap. City councils make 780+ decisions per month across the Bay Area, buried in PDFs and fragmented platforms. Residents affected by these decisions—zoning changes, budget cuts, shelter sitings—rarely know about them until it's too late, and even when they do, they can't find others who share their concerns.

**Our solution:** We've built extraction pipelines for 26 cities, transcript search over council meetings (turning 5-hour videos into instant lookups), and a coordination workflow to route affected residents to relevant decisions. Operational cost: <$7/month.

**Why open source matters:** Civic infrastructure should be a public good. By keeping this MIT-licensed, any region can deploy their own instance. We're building the "Wikipedia of local democracy data"—freely available, community-maintained, and impossible for any single entity to capture.

**What MOSS funding enables:**
- Expand transcript processing to more cities
- Improve speaker diarization and attribution
- Build the commitment tracker (accountability for council promises)
- Run and document the January 2026 pilot
- Create deployment guides for other regions
