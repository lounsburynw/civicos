## Decision Timing (AI-Critical)

### Planning & Development
- **Planning permits**: 3-6 months average processing time
- **Environmental review**: Varies by project complexity (CEQA compliance required)
- **Zoning changes**: Planning Commission → Board of Supervisors (4-8 months)
- **Major developments**: 1-2+ years with extensive community input process
- **Appeal periods**: 10 days after Planning Commission decisions

### Budget Process
- **Budget season**: February-June annually 
- **Public hearings**: May-June for following fiscal year (July-June)
- **Department presentations**: April-May budget workshops
- **Final adoption**: June before July 1 fiscal year start
- **Mid-year budget adjustments**: December-January

### Meeting Schedules
- **Board of Supervisors**: Tuesdays, 9:00 AM (2-3 meetings per month)
- **Planning Commission**: 1st and 3rd Thursday, 1:00 PM
- **Budget workshops**: Special schedule April-June
- **Study sessions**: As needed for complex policy issues

### Agenda Deadlines
- **Agenda posting**: 72 hours before meetings (Brown Act requirement)
- **Public comment submission**: Email BOS@marincounty.gov by Monday noon for Tuesday meetings
- **Written testimony**: Submit to Clerk of Board for official record
- **Emergency items**: Special procedures for urgent matters

## Jurisdictional Authority

### Marin County Boundaries (Unincorporated Areas + Regional Services)
- **Geographic Coverage**: All unincorporated areas OUTSIDE city limits
- **Examples**: Lucas Valley, West Marin, Santa Venetia, unincorporated Novato areas
- **NOT COVERED**: Inside incorporated cities (San Rafael, Mill Valley, etc.) → City jurisdiction

### County Exclusive Authority
- **Unincorporated areas only**: Zoning, building permits, planning, code enforcement
- **Countywide services**: Sheriff, public health, social services, superior courts
- **Regional infrastructure**: County roads, bridges, flood control, regional parks
- **Special districts governance**: Open Space, Flood Control, Housing Authority boards

### Regional Coordination Required
- **Water**: Multiple water districts (MMWD, North Marin, etc.) - separate governance
- **Transportation**: Golden Gate Transit, BART (county not in district), TAM coordination
- **Housing**: ABAG regional housing needs allocation compliance
- **Environmental**: Bay Area Air Quality Management District, SF Bay Conservation Commission

### Appeal Processes
- **Planning decisions**: Planning Commission → Board of Supervisors → Courts
- **Building permits**: Staff → Planning Commission → Board of Supervisors
- **Environmental review**: Administrative appeals through CEQA process
