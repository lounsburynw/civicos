# Civic Feature Guide

This guide explains each Civic feature in detail. For a quick overview, see [Getting Started](GETTING_STARTED.md).

---

## Overview

Civic helps you participate in local government through three types of features:

| Category | What It Does | Features |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Learn** | Understand what's happening | What's Next, What Applies, What Happened, Who's With Me |
| **Act** | Take action | Add Your Voice, Start Something, Follow, Prepare |
| **Ask** | Get AI assistance | Chat with the Civic Assistant |

---

## Learn Features

### What's Next

**Purpose:** See upcoming city council meetings and what's being decided.

**When to use:**
- You want to know what's happening in local government this month
- You're looking for meetings about a specific topic (housing, transportation, etc.)
- You want to find opportunities to participate before decisions are made

**What you'll see:**
- Meeting date, time, and location
- List of agenda items with descriptions
- Topic tags for filtering (Housing, Transportation, Budget, etc.)

**How to use it:**

1. **Browse the Events panel** (left side of the screen) to see upcoming meetings
2. **Click any meeting** to see its full agenda
3. **Filter by topic** using the topic buttons at the top
4. **Look ahead** — meetings are shown up to 30 days in the future

**Example questions for the Assistant:**
- *"What's being decided about housing this month?"*
- *"Are there any transportation meetings coming up?"*
- *"What's on the agenda for the next city council meeting?"*

**Tips:**
- Meetings are sorted by date, with the nearest first
- Each agenda item shows its topic category (Housing, Development, Budget, etc.)
- Click an agenda item to see staff reports and background materials

### What Applies

**Purpose:** Understand the laws and regulations relevant to any topic.

**When to use:**
- You want to know what rules govern a topic before speaking
- You're researching the legal context for an issue
- You want to understand federal, state, and local requirements

**What you'll see:**

The regulatory stack shows three levels:

| Level | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Federal** | HUD programs, FEMA requirements, federal grants |
| **State** | California housing bills, environmental mandates |
| **Local** | San Rafael municipal code, city ordinances |

**How to use it:**

1. **Ask the Assistant:** *"What laws apply to housing in San Rafael?"*
2. **Browse the Legislative panel** to explore bills and programs
3. **Click any legislation** to see details, status, and relevance

**Example questions for the Assistant:**
- *"What state laws affect affordable housing?"*
- *"What federal programs fund homelessness prevention?"*
- *"What does the city code say about ADUs?"*

**Tips:**
- State bills show their current status (introduced, passed, signed)
- Federal programs include funding amounts and eligibility
- Local ordinances link to the official municipal code

### What Happened

**Purpose:** Search past council decisions and meeting discussions.

**When to use:**
- You want to know how the council voted on a topic
- You're researching what residents said about an issue
- You want to find precedents before testifying

**What you'll see:**

| Type | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Decisions** | Council votes, outcomes, staff recommendations |
| **Transcripts** | What was said in meetings (public testimony, council discussion) |
| **Video clips** | Links to meeting recordings with timestamps |

**How to use it:**

**For decisions (what was voted on):**
1. Ask: *"What has the council decided about bike lanes?"*
2. Results show the vote, outcome, and meeting date
3. Click any decision to see the full context

**For spoken content (what was said):**
1. Ask: *"What have residents said about traffic on 4th Street?"*
2. Results show transcript excerpts with speaker names
3. Click the video link to watch the original recording

**For complete context (decisions + discussion):**
1. Ask: *"Tell me everything about the homeless shelter decision"*
2. Results combine the official vote with staff presentations and public testimony

**Example questions for the Assistant:**
- *"What decisions were made about housing in 2024?"*
- *"What did the public say about the new development?"*
- *"How did the council vote on the bike lane proposal?"*

**Understanding transcript results:**

Each transcript excerpt shows:
- **Speaker role:** Council member, staff, or public
- **Speaker name:** When identified from the video
- **Timestamp:** When it was said in the meeting
- **Video link:** Click to watch the original moment

**Tips:**
- Use specific keywords for better results
- Filter by date if you're looking for recent decisions
- Public testimony excerpts are marked separately from staff/council discussion

### Who's With Me

**Purpose:** Find neighbors who care about the same issues.

**When to use:**
- You want to see if others share your concerns
- You're looking for allies before a meeting
- You want to understand community interest in a topic

**What you'll see:**
- Number of people following the topic
- Recent issues filed about related concerns
- Active initiatives you can support

**How to use it:**

1. **Click on any issue or meeting** to see related community activity
2. **Ask the Assistant:** *"Who else cares about traffic safety?"*
3. **Browse initiatives** to find organized efforts you can join

**Tips:**
- Related issues use smart matching — "traffic safety" finds "speeding," "crosswalks," and "bike lanes"
- The more specific your topic, the more relevant the matches
- Finding allies makes participation less intimidating

## Act Features

### Add Your Voice

**Purpose:** Express your opinion on a proposal, agenda item, or initiative.

**When to use:**
- You want to support or oppose something being decided
- You have a question for the council to consider
- You want your perspective recorded before a vote

**How it works:**

1. **Find the item** — an agenda item, decision, or initiative
2. **Click "Add Voice"** or the comment button
3. **Choose your stance:**
   - **Support** — You're in favor
   - **Oppose** — You're against
   - **Question** — You have a question (not taking a side)
4. **Write your comment** — Explain your reasoning
5. **Submit** — Your voice is recorded

**What happens next:**
- Your comment becomes part of the public record
- Others can see community sentiment on the item
- The council receives public input before voting

**Tips:**
- Be specific about why you support or oppose
- Questions are valuable — they can highlight issues the council hasn't considered
- Your comment is public, just like speaking at a meeting

---

### Start Something

**Purpose:** Create a new initiative for others to support.

**When to use:**
- You have an idea that needs community backing
- You want to organize neighbors around a specific proposal
- You've identified a problem and have a potential solution

**How it works:**

1. **Click "Start Initiative"** (or use keyboard shortcut `Cmd/Ctrl + I`)
2. **Choose a topic:** Housing, Transportation, Environment, Budget, etc.
3. **Write a title:** Clear, specific (e.g., "Protected bike lane on 4th Street")
4. **Add a description:** Explain the problem and your proposed solution
5. **Set a location** (optional): If it's about a specific place
6. **Submit**

**What happens next:**
- Your initiative appears in the community feed
- Others can add their voices (support, oppose, question)
- When you reach enough supporters, Civic suggests coordination options

**Tips:**
- Check "Who's With Me" first — there might already be a similar initiative
- Specific titles get more engagement than vague ones
- Include the "why" in your description, not just the "what"

---

### Follow

**Purpose:** Get notified about things you care about.

**When to use:**
- You found a meeting or initiative you want to track
- You want to know when a decision is made
- You don't want to miss an upcoming opportunity to participate

**What you can follow:**

| Type | You'll be notified when... |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Meeting** | Agenda is updated, date approaches, decisions are made |
| **Initiative** | New supporters join, updates are posted |
| **Topic** | New meetings or initiatives match your interest |
| **Decision** | Outcome is reported, related items appear |

**How to use it:**

1. **Find the item** you want to follow
2. **Click the "Follow" button** (heart or bell icon)
3. **Check your notifications** for updates

**Tips:**
- Follow before you forget — if something catches your eye, click Follow
- You can follow topics broadly ("housing") or items specifically
- Unfollow anytime by clicking the button again

---

### Prepare

**Purpose:** Get help preparing for effective participation.

**When to use:**
- You're planning to speak at a meeting
- You want to write a public comment
- You need background information before testifying

**What you'll get:**

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Regulatory Context** | Relevant laws and regulations |
| **Historical Decisions** | How the council has voted on similar issues |
| **Talking Points** | Key arguments based on the agenda item |
| **Allies** | Others who've expressed similar views |
| **Logistics** | Meeting time, location, speaking procedures |

**How to use it:**

1. **Find the agenda item** you want to speak about
2. **Click "Prepare"** (or ask the Assistant)
3. **Review the materials** Civic generates
4. **Draft your comment** using the AI-assisted editor

**Example requests for the Assistant:**
- *"Help me prepare a comment about the bike lane proposal"*
- *"What should I know before speaking about the housing development?"*
- *"Draft a public comment supporting the traffic calming project"*

**Tips:**
- Preparation works best when you're specific about which agenda item
- Review historical decisions to understand precedents
- Talking points are suggestions — personalize them with your own experience

## Ask Features

### Chat with the Civic Assistant

**Purpose:** Get answers and help using natural language.

**When to use:**
- You have questions about local government
- You want help finding specific information
- You prefer asking questions to clicking through menus

**Where to find it:**
- The chat panel on the right side of the screen
- Type your question naturally, like you'd ask a neighbor

**What the Assistant can do:**

| Task | Example Question |
|------|------------------|
| **Find information** | *"What's happening with housing this month?"* |
| **Search history** | *"Has the council discussed 4th Street traffic?"* |
| **Explain context** | *"What laws apply to ADUs in San Rafael?"* |
| **Draft comments** | *"Help me write a comment supporting the bike lane"* |
| **Prepare for meetings** | *"What should I know about the homeless shelter item?"* |

**How it works:**

1. **Type your question** in natural language
2. **The Assistant searches** city records, transcripts, and regulations
3. **You get an answer** based on real civic data
4. **Ask follow-ups** — the Assistant remembers the conversation

**Tips:**
- Be specific: *"housing on Lincoln Avenue"* works better than just *"housing"*
- Ask follow-ups: *"Tell me more about that"* or *"When was that decided?"*
- The Assistant can't make things up — answers come from city data

**What the Assistant can't do:**
- Predict future votes or outcomes
- Speak to the council on your behalf
- Access non-public information

## Privacy and Public Records

### Understanding What's Public

Civic follows the same rules as attending a city council meeting:

| Action | Privacy Level |
|--------|---------------|
| **Browsing** | Private — only you see what you look at |
| **Following** | Private — your subscriptions aren't shared |
| **Adding a voice** | Public — comments become part of the public record |
| **Starting an initiative** | Public — visible to all users |

### Data You Control

- **Personal context:** Information you share to improve comment drafts (optional)
- **Notification preferences:** How and when you receive updates
- **Privacy tier:** Choose how much you share with other users

**To adjust your privacy settings:** Click your profile icon and select "Privacy Settings"

## Troubleshooting

### Common Questions

**Why don't I see any upcoming meetings?**
Meetings are loaded from the city's Legistar system. If no meetings appear, there may be none scheduled in the next 30 days, or the data source may be temporarily unavailable.

**My search returned no results**
Try broader terms or different keywords. For example, instead of "protected bicycle infrastructure," try "bike lanes."

**The video timestamp doesn't match**
Timestamps are approximate and may be off by a few seconds. The video will start near the relevant content.

**I can't find a past decision**
Civic's records go back to when data collection began. Very old decisions may not be available.
